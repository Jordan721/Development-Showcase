Project story / 00

An old idea,
rebuilt with new skills.

The One Day Triathlon began as an academic project built around a simple question: what could someone create if they had only one day to make a game?

The original website introduced the game-jam concept, explained the challenge, and offered a list of genres for participants to choose from. Its goal was to encourage experimentation under pressure, reflect the realities of creative deadlines, and give people a reason to share feedback with one another.

Years later, I returned to the project to see how far my skills had developed. I kept its playful spirit and 24-hour premise, then rebuilt the experience from the ground up as a focused, interactive event site.

ThenAcademic prototype

A simple informational website

  • Separate home, about, and genre pages
  • Bootstrap-based page layouts
  • Static genre descriptions
  • Early experiments with visual design
NowModern rebuild

An interactive game-jam experience

  • Responsive, custom visual system
  • Dynamic challenge generator with lock controls
  • Persistent 24-hour race clock
  • Accessible navigation and reduced-motion support

This rebuild is both a tribute to where I started and a snapshot of how I design and build for the web today.

The format / 02

A creative triathlon.

Good game jams are about momentum, not perfection. Move through three focused stages and protect the final hours for a playable build.

01

Hours 00-02

Concept sprint

Find the smallest fun idea hiding inside the brief. Sketch the loop, choose your tools, and define what "done" means.

02

Hours 02-20

Build the core

Make the interaction work first. Test early, trim aggressively, and keep the game playable as it grows.

Race rules / 03

Keep it fast. Keep it fair.

01

Start fresh

Existing tools and asset libraries are welcome. The game itself begins when the clock does.

02

Honor the brief

Your genre, mechanic, and wildcard should all be recognizable in the final experience.

03

Build together

Solo racers and small teams compete on equal ground. Share knowledge freely along the way.

04

Ship something

Submit a playable build, a short description, and one screenshot before time expires.
