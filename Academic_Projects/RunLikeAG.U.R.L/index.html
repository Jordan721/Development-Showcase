Historical context / 02

From reaction
to representation.

  1. 2016

    A political turning point

    The presidential election becomes a catalyst for new organizing, candidacies, and conversations about representation.

  2. 2017

    Mobilization becomes visible

    Women’s marches and local organizing networks turn public response into sustained political participation.

  3. 2018

    A record-setting election cycle

    Women run for office in unprecedented numbers, while campaigns and organizers confront questions of access, identity, and electability.

  4. Today

    The archive looks back

    This redesign preserves the project as a historical snapshot and invites new reflection on the questions it raised.

Themes / 03

Four lenses on
the same race.

01 / 04

Who gets to lead?

The film asks what changes when more women enter races for public office, and what representation means beyond a single election result.

02 / 04

A different code

Women candidates are often expected to appear decisive but not aggressive, knowledgeable but not condescending, and ambitious without seeming self-serving.

03 / 04

Campaigns are collective

Phone banks, canvassing, community groups, and volunteer networks reveal the sustained labor behind political momentum.

04 / 04

The vote is not automatic

The documentary also examines how women voters evaluate candidates and why shared identity does not always translate into shared political support.

Project evolution / 06

Reframing an
academic project.

This began as a student group project created after the original Run Like A G.U.R.L WordPress site was no longer available.

My role was to build the website while other group members developed the artwork and final documentation. The first version preserved the organization’s content in a conventional promotional layout with news, social media, donation, and contact sections.

I returned to the project to make its purpose clearer and demonstrate how my approach has changed. The new version treats the work as an educational archive: it prioritizes the film, historical context, accessible storytelling, and transparent authorship.
