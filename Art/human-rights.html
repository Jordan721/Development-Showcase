About This Project

Why Gun Violence?

This assignment asked us to choose a human rights article and create a poster around it. I chose Article 3, which states that everyone has the right to life, liberty, and security of person. The reason I focused on gun violence is because of the epidemic that has been sweeping the nation — from schools to public spaces, the numbers speak for themselves. This poster was my way of saying: enough is enough.
