Art History Montage

Alexis — 2018

A digital montage capturing the essence of Norman Rockwell's enduring influence on American art. Through collage and composition, this piece explores Rockwell's ability to immortalize everyday American life and the universal pursuit of the "American Dream."

The Inspiration

Norman Rockwell

Norman Rockwell was one of America's most beloved illustrators, known for his depictions of everyday American life that graced the covers of The Saturday Evening Post for nearly half a century.

I chose Rockwell because of his incredible ability to inspire generations of artists to capture the warmth, humor, and humanity of ordinary moments — the very essence of the American Dream.
