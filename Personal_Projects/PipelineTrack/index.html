Head to My Profile and add your skills. This is what powers fit scoring — without it, all fit scores will show 0%.

Add your first job

Hit + Add Job on the Job Board. Click ⚡ Paste full listing and drop in the raw job posting — it'll auto-fill role, company, location, salary, and more, and move the cleaned description to Job Info automatically.

Fields that were guessed show a ✦ Auto-filled tag — always review them before saving. Parsing is best-effort and will keep improving. Matched skills are highlighted green and gaps in red inside the description.
