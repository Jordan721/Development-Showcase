How to Play
Get started in seconds

How to Play

01
Pick a Shape

Choose one of 8 preloaded shapes from the sidebar, or upload your own .obj file.

02
Select Your Tool

Pick from Hammer, Bomb, Shockwave, or Energy Blast. Adjust force with the slider.

03
Left Click to Destroy

Click directly on the shape. Each hit chips off debris and drains its integrity bar.

04
Collect Powerups

When a shape shatters, glowing orbs fly out. Click them before they disappear!

05
Right Drag to Orbit

Hold right mouse and drag to rotate the camera around the shape. Scroll to zoom.

06
Chain Combos

Hit rapidly to build your ×8 combo multiplier. Time out means combo resets!
