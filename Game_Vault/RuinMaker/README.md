## 🎮 What is RuinMaker?

**RuinMaker** is a physics-based 3D destruction sandbox where your only goal is to **break things** as satisfyingly as possible. Load up a shape, grab a tool, and unleash chaos — fragments fly, sparks burst, and powerups rain down from the wreckage!

No building. No crafting. No survival. Just pure, glorious **destruction**. 🔨

## 🚀 How to Play

![Gameplay GIF](https://media3.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExaDNhMXJlZGN1ZWVldWQxa3Vpa2s3aTdvdGptazJ0MDZ4bXM2YWI3aiZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/lRv7XL9l5xuFO/giphy.gif)

### Step 1 — Pick a Shape
Click any shape in the **left sidebar** to load it into the scene. Or upload your own `.obj` file!

### Step 2 — Choose Your Weapon
Select one of 4 tools from the **right sidebar**:

| Tool | Effect |
|---|---|
| 🔨 **Hammer** | Precision strike — click to smash exactly where you aim |
| 💣 **Bomb** | Area explosion — obliterates everything nearby |
| 🌀 **Shockwave** | Radial pulse — sends fragments flying outward |
| ⚡ **Energy Blast** | High-damage beam — melts through tough materials |

### Step 3 — Destroy!
**Left-click** on the 3D object to strike it. Watch it shatter into fragments that bounce, tumble, and fade!

### Step 4 — Collect Powerups
Glowing orbs drop from the wreckage. **Click them** before they disappear!

## 📂 Upload Your Own Models

Got a `.obj` file lying around? Destroy it!

```
1. Click "Upload OBJ" in the sidebar
2. Select any .obj file from your computer
3. Watch it appear in the scene
4. Immediately destroy it 💣
```

> Works with any standard OBJ file. No MTL needed — pure geometry destruction!

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

- 🎯 **Aim for the center** — core hits generate more fragments
- ⚡ **Combo powerups** — Time Slow + Multi-Break = 3 slow-mo hits at once
- 🔊 **Use the Force slider** — max force = maximum carnage
- 🐰 **Try the Bunny** — it's got the most satisfying shatter pattern
- 💾 **Import complex OBJs** — the more vertices, the bigger the explosion
