TYPE TO SURVIVE

A dungeon lies beneath. Monsters guard every corridor. Your only weapon is your keyboard. Type fast, type true, or die in the dark.

HOW TO PLAY

EXPLORE Move with WASD or arrow keys. Click tiles to move. Uncover the dungeon room by room.
FIGHT Step on enemies to engage. Type the words to attack. Speed and accuracy deal more damage.
COLLECT Open chests for ability cards. Heal, shield, double strike, freeze time, or summon lightning.
DESCEND Find the exit to go deeper. Each floor gets harder. How far can you survive?
