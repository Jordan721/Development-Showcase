## 🕹️ What is Game Vault?

**Game Vault** is a retro-styled arcade hub showcasing browser-based games I've created. From survival horror to rhythm games, each project represents different aspects of game development — all playable directly in your browser.

## 🎮 Game Descriptions

### 💀 The Unfair Game
*Nothing is as it seems.*

Survive invisible danger zones, evade hunters, and reach the exit. A survival horror puzzle game where trust is your enemy.

### 🔥 The Hard Hallway (THW)
*Trust nothing. Die constantly.*

A brutally unfair 2D shooter inspired by "I Wanna Be The Guy". Expect the unexpected — every corner hides death.

### 📖 The Idea Space
*A digital demon compendium.*

Explore, compare, and discover demons across the Shin Megami Tensei series timeline. An interactive database tribute to the SMT franchise.

### 🎵 Beat Burst
*Your music, your game.*

Upload any song and transform it into a rhythm game. Visualize the beats, then play along to your own music.

### ⌨️ Typebound Dungeon
*Type to survive.*

Explore procedurally generated dungeons, fight monsters with your keyboard, and collect ability cards. The faster you type, the deadlier you are.

### 💥 RuinMaker
*If it ain't broken, you haven't tried hard enough.*

A physics-based 3D destruction sandbox. Load a shape (or upload your own OBJ), grab a tool, and obliterate everything. Collect powerups from the wreckage and hit score targets in Challenge Mode.

## 🕹️ Secret Terminal Commands

Press `~` to open the terminal, then try these commands:

```
help      - Show available commands
games     - List all games
stats     - View dev statistics
snake     - Play Snake
clear     - Clear terminal
exit      - Close terminal
```
